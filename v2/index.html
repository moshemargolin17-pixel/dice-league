What the columns mean

W and L are wins and losses. PCT is win percentage. GB is games behind first place. Home and Away are records in home and away games. Div, Conf and Inter are records against the player's division, conference and the other conference. PF/G and PA/G are points scored and allowed per game, and Diff is the difference. L10 is the record in the last 10 games. Strk is the current winning or losing streak.

A solid line falls under the 6th seed (playoffs) and a dashed line under the 10th (play-in). Ties are broken by head-to-head record, then division or conference record, then point differential.
